**Whats New:**
- Stemming has been added with complete suffix rules
- - ``"ization"`` to ``"ize"`` (and ``"isation"`` to ``"ize"``)
- - ``"fulness"`` to ``"ful"``
- - ``"iveness"`` to ``"ive"``
- - ``"ousness"`` to ``"ous"``
- - ``"aliti"`` to ``"al"``
- - ``"iviti"`` to ``"ive"``
- - ``"biliti"`` to ``"ble"``
- - ``"logi"`` to ``"log"``
- Irregular Word Handling
- - Plurals: ``mice to mouse``, ``children to child``, ``oxen to ox``
- - Verb past tense: ``ran to run``, ``went to go``, ``saw to see``
- - Special cases: ``data to datum``, ``criteria to criterion``
- Enhanced Verb Conjugation Rules
- - Better handling of double consonants (``stopped`` to ``stop``)
- - Proper ``-ed/-ing`` suffix removal
- - ``at/bl/iz`` suffix addition rules
- Additional Suffix Coverage
- - ``"ance"``, ``"ence"``, ``"ment"`` in multiple processing steps
- - ``"isation"`` variant for British spelling
- - ``"tion"`` and ``"ion"`` handling
